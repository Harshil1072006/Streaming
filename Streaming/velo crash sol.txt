Sometimes, corrupted cache files can prevent the client from launching or logging in correctly.

1.  Make sure all Riot and Valorant processes are closed using the method in Option 1.

### Option 4: Reinstall Riot Vanguard (This is the most likely solution)

Riot Vanguard is the kernel-level anti-cheat. When it gets corrupted, it can cause exactly the kind of "stuck on startup" issue you are describing. A simple restart doesn't fix it because it loads with Windows itself.

1.  Press the `Windows Key`, type `Add or remove programs`, and press Enter.

7.  **Important:** You will need to **restart your computer one more time** to finalize the Vanguard installation.

### Option 5: Flush your DNS and Reset Network Settings

An internet outage can sometimes mess up your computer's saved network data. Clearing this can help.

1.  Press the `Windows Key`, type `cmd`.
2.  Right-click on **"Command Prompt"** and select **"Run as administrator"**.
3.  Type the following commands, pressing Enter after each one:
    * `ipconfig /flushdns`
    * `netsh winsock reset`
4.  After running both commands, restart your PC.
